# MDM for one hand-enrolled Mac mini (Lightning Jar), researched 2026-11-05

Target: 1 × Mac mini M2 Pro, macOS 27.2, leased from MacStadium (Dublin), enrolled by hand (user-approved
Device Enrollment, not ADE). Needs: (1) Apps and Books device-assigned Logic Pro, no Apple ID;
(2) Restrictions payload with `forceBypassScreenCaptureAlert = true`, PPPC nice to have; (3) works with manual
enrollment of a Mac not in ABM; (4) cheap, low effort, reputable.

Legend: **[V]** verified on the vendor's or Apple's own page today. **[3P]** third-party aggregator only.
**[OLD]** source more than about a year old. **[U]** unverified or inferred.

## 0. Big change: Apple Business Manager is now "Apple Business" (since 14 April 2026)

- Apple announced on 24 March 2026 that Apple Business Manager, Apple Business Essentials and Apple Business
  Connect would merge into one free platform, **Apple Business**, launching 14 April 2026. It includes a
  **free built-in MDM**, and Essentials customers stop paying their per-device fee. **[V]**
  https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/03/introducing-apple-business-a-new-all-in-one-platform-for-businesses-of-all-sizes/
- So the "ABM" approval Kevin has is now an Apple Business account, and Apple Business Essentials no longer
  exists as a paid product. Console paths have changed. Content tokens, for example, are now under
  Devices > Management Services. **[V]** https://support.apple.com/guide/business/manage-content-tokens-axme0f8659ec/web

## 1. The hard constraint: the key itself

From Apple's device-management schema (`com.apple.applicationaccess.yaml`, release branch), `forceBypassScreenCaptureAlert`
was **introduced in macOS 15.1**, has `allowmanualinstall: false` (MDM only), and has `userenrollment: forbidden`. It has **no
`supervised: true` requirement**, so a user-approved, unsupervised Device Enrollment qualifies. **[V]**
https://github.com/apple/device-management/blob/release/mdm/profiles/com.apple.applicationaccess.yaml

As a result, any MDM that can push a **custom .mobileconfig as a device-level profile** over a Device Enrollment
(not User Enrollment) can send it. No product needs a built-in toggle for this key. PPPC (`com.apple.TCC.configuration-profile-policy`)
works the same way. Note that PPPC can't pre-grant Screen Recording; it can only let standard users approve it.

## 3. Recommendation

**Recommended: SimpleMDM, at $3/month billed monthly (about $36/yr; $30/yr if billed annually), hosted.**
- It meets all three hard requirements on its standard plan, with no tier gating. Profiles go out at device level, Apps and Books
  licences are assigned to the device by default with no Apple ID, and enrollment is an ordinary user-approved enrollment link.
- No minimum, no contract, and the 30-day trial is enough to prove the profile and the Logic Pro install before paying. It is a
  long-established Mac-admin vendor with a public API (handy if agents should drive it later).

**Runner-up: Jamf Now (possibly $0).** If the 3 free devices still include the Plus features (custom profiles), it costs nothing.
Otherwise it costs about $4/mo. Confirm in the trial that "Custom Profiles" appears in the blueprint before relying on it.

**Why not the free Apple Business built-in MDM?** It's free and can upload the custom profile. But a hand-enrolled
Mac has to enroll through **account-driven Device Enrollment, which requires signing a Managed Apple Account in on the Mac**,
and device-level Blueprints are ADE-only. That conflicts with the "no Apple ID signed in" goal for an unattended test box.
Use it only if a dedicated Managed Apple Account signed in on the mini is acceptable. A bonus: on macOS that path makes the
Mac *supervised*. Whether apps reach it through a device licence or a user licence there is **[U]**.

## 4. Setup steps (SimpleMDM + Apple Business)

1. **Apple Business account hygiene.** Create a dedicated Managed Apple Account (for example `mdm-tokens@…`) with a custom role
   limited to content/licence management. Apple recommends this so a password change doesn't invalidate the token. Content tokens
   expire after **1 year**. [V] https://support.apple.com/guide/business/manage-content-tokens-axme0f8659ec/web
2. **SimpleMDM trial.** Sign up (30 days, all features). Create the **APNs push certificate** in SimpleMDM: it gives you a CSR,
   you upload it at identity.apple.com/pushcert with a company Apple Account, and upload the .pem back. It renews yearly and
   must use the **same Apple Account** each time. [U, standard Apple flow]
3. **Device-management server in Apple Business (optional for this Mac).** Devices > Management Services > add an external service,
   upload SimpleMDM's public key, download the server token, and upload it to SimpleMDM. This matters only for ADE devices. A hand-enrolled
   MacStadium Mac is never assigned there, so you can skip this step for now. [V path]
   https://support.apple.com/guide/business/intro-to-device-management-services-axm659f6bd48/web
4. **Apps and Books content token.** In Apple Business, Devices > Management Services > (service) > download the content token
   (some regional guides still say Settings > Payments & Billing). Upload it to SimpleMDM (Apps > Apps and Books). Make sure device-level
   assignment is on (Apps > Settings). Don't also connect the same location's token to the built-in service. Apple warns this
   causes licence-inventory conflicts. [V] (content-token page above)
5. **Buy Logic Pro licences.** In Apple Business Apps and Books, search Logic Pro and buy 1 licence: **$199.99** (US App Store,
   v13.0, needs macOS 26.1+) [V] https://apps.apple.com/us/app/logic-pro/id634148309?mt=12 .
   **Payment: yes, a credit or debit card works.** An Org Admin or Content Manager can add an individual card, or an Org Admin can add one shared
   card. VPP Credit (purchase order) is the alternative. Gift cards are not accepted. [V]
   https://support.apple.com/guide/business/review-app-book-payment-billing-information-axm746a59fa2/web ,
   https://support.apple.com/en-us/102867
6. **Build profiles** (iMazing Profile Editor or hand-written XML). Make a Restrictions payload `com.apple.applicationaccess` with
   `forceBypassScreenCaptureAlert` `<true/>`, and a separate PPPC profile for the automation clients (sshd-keygen-wrapper / Terminal /
   agent binaries → Accessibility, AppleEvents, etc.). Upload each in SimpleMDM as a custom profile, **ticking device-level**.
7. **Enroll the Mac by hand.** Over Screen Sharing (GUI needed once), open the SimpleMDM enrollment link, download the profile, and approve it in
   System Settings > General > Device Management. This is user-approved Device Enrollment, so it is "MDM approved" but not supervised.
8. **Assign** the profiles and the Logic Pro app to the device's group. Verify with `sudo profiles show -type configuration` and
   `profiles status -type enrollment` (expect "MDM enrollment: Yes (User Approved)"). Confirm Logic Pro installs with no Apple ID signed in.
9. **Calendar the renewals:** APNs certificate (yearly), content token (yearly), SimpleMDM card.

## 5. Flags and open questions

- **Cross-country licence use [U]:** Lightning Jar is a US org buying from the US storefront, and the Mac sits in Ireland. I couldn't find a
  current Apple page on whether device-assigned licences install outside the purchasing country. Test it during the trial before buying more.
- **Logic Pro 13 needs macOS 26.1+.** macOS 27.2 is fine.
- **MacStadium:** confirm the lease terms allow installing an MDM enrollment profile, and that MacStadium doesn't already enroll the
  machine in its own MDM. [U, not researched]
- Jamf Now's current per-device price is shown as "starts at $4". The Standard and Plus split and the free-tier feature set are [OLD/3P].
- Mosyle FREE feature scope, Iru pricing and Hexnode per-tier contents are [3P] or [U].
- The SimpleMDM feature pages and the device-assignment blog are older [OLD], but device assignment is a long-standing default. Verify in the trial.
