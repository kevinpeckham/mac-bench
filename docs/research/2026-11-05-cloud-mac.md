# Cloud Mac for automated Dorico / Ableton / Logic testing: research and recommendation

Date: 2026-11-05. Prepared for Kevin Peckham (Lightning Jar). Nothing was signed up for, bought or contacted.

Every price and hard constraint has a source number (see **Sources** at the end). "Unverified" means I could not
reach a primary source (several provider sites returned 403 to the fetcher) and the figure comes from a
secondary listing or a competitor's comparison page.

---

## TL;DR

- **The single deciding constraint is Logic Pro.** It is a Mac App Store app, and App Store apps do not run in
  Apple-silicon macOS VMs: even on macOS 15+ (where Apple ID/iCloud sign-in in a VM became possible), the Mac App
  Store sign-in is still unsupported and "most of its apps will simply refuse to run" [S14][S15][S16]. Logic also
  needs macOS 15.6+ on Apple silicon [S20]. **So the machine must be bare-metal macOS on Apple silicon**, which
  rules out Orka, Tart/UTM/Parallels VMs, VM-based "dedicated" plans and GitHub-hosted runners.
- **Recommendation: rent a bare-metal Mac mini month-to-month from MacStadium, plan M4.M (M4 10-core, 24 GB,
  512 GB SSD) at $249/month** [S8]. US-hosted, full root, preconfigured Screen Sharing, monthly billing.
  Step up to **M4.L (M4 Pro, 48 GB, 1 TB) at $349/month** if Logic/Ableton content libraries or parallel jobs
  need it [S8].
- **Runner-up (cheapest over a year or more): buy a Mac mini (M6, 24 GB, 1 TB ≈ $1,599 list) and run it at
  home/office (≈ $1/month power) or in a US colo (≈ $50/month)** [S24][S25][S27]. It breaks even against
  MacStadium M4.M after about 7 months at home or about 8 months in colo, and it is the only option where
  the audio hardware (built-in speaker, headphone jack) and an HDMI dummy plug are certainly under your control.
- **EU alternative with better specs per euro: Scaleway M4-M (M4, 32 GB, 1 TB) at €199/month** (≈ $230;
  unverified, pricing page blocked) [S10][S11]; **OakHost M4Pro.L (M4 Pro, 64 GB, 1 TB) €260/month**, Germany [S12].
- **AWS EC2 Mac works but is a poor fit:** mac-m4 is $1.23/h ≈ $898/month + EBS (≈ $90/month) [S2][S6], with
  FileVault forbidden, boot from EBS, up to 4.5 h scrub after every stop, and no documented audio device [S3][S4][S5].

## 2. Per-option notes

### 2.1 MacStadium bare-metal Mac mini (recommended)
- Plans and prices (official pricing page, fetched 2026-11-05) [S8]: M4.S $149 (M4/16 GB/256 GB), **M4.M $249
  (M4/24 GB/512 GB)**, M4.L $349 (M4 Pro 12-core/48 GB/1 TB); also M2-era and Mac Studio plans. Billing: "flexible
  monthly or annual"; monthly subscriptions prepaid on the first of the month; 1 Gbps, unlimited bandwidth; "full root
  access" [S8]. No M6/M5 Pro plans listed yet.
- Access: Screen Sharing is preconfigured; **inbound TCP 5900 is blocked at MacStadium's network edge** and they
  recommend an SSH tunnel [S9]. For us that's moot: install Tailscale and use Screen Sharing over the tailnet.
- Gotcha: MacStadium's own docs moved to docs.macstadium.com in April 2026; older support articles may be stale [S9].
- Gotcha: an M4 Mac mini can run macOS 15 (Sequoia), which is what Kevin's MacBook runs today (15.7). Ask MacStadium
  which macOS they image with and whether they will install 15.x (Logic needs 15.6+ [S20]).

### 2.2 Scaleway Apple silicon (Paris)
- M4-S €149/month (16 GB/256 GB), €0.22/h; **M4-M €199/month (32 GB/1 TB)**, €0.29/h; M4 Pro 64 GB/2 TB €335/month,
  €0.49/h [S10][S11]. Scaleway's pricing page and docs returned 403 to the fetcher, so these are from a WHTop listing
  (dated 2026-04-05), a competitor's page citing Scaleway "as of June 2026", and Scaleway signup/French pricing pages
  as summarized by search: **unverified**.
- 24-hour minimum on hourly billing (EULA) [S10][S11]; VNC available from the Scaleway console [S11].
- Best RAM/storage per dollar among the big names, but EU-hosted and VAT-exclusive pricing.

### 2.3 OakHost (Germany)
- Official price table (fetched): M4.M (M4/32 GB/1 TB) €135 — **out of stock**; M6.M (M6/32 GB/1 TB) €200; M4Pro.L
  (M4 Pro/64 GB/1 TB) €260; M5Pro.L (64 GB/1 TB) €340. Dedicated Mac mini, static IP, KVM remote management, full root,
  "Apple ID sign-in supported", App Store installs mentioned, 14-day money-back on first monthly order [S12].
- Minimum term and setup fee not stated on the page.

### 2.4 HostMyApple
- M4/16 GB/256 GB $119.99; M6/16 GB/256 GB $139.99; M6/32 GB/256 GB $179.99; full root; VNC/NoMachine/ARD; term and
  data-centre location for dedicated minis not stated [S13]. **256 GB is too small** once Logic's sound library and
  Ableton packs are installed (see sizing).

### 2.5 MacinCloud
- Official site returned 403/404 to the fetcher. A competitor's page quotes MacinCloud Dedicated Server **M4 at $124.99/month
  or $1,249/year** "as of June 2026", 100 Mbps, full root on dedicated plans [S18]; another competitor claims some
  MacinCloud "dedicated" offerings are VMware VMs on Intel hardware [S18b]. **Unverified; confirm bare-metal Apple
  silicon and RAM/storage before considering.** If it is a VM, Logic is out.

### 2.6 Flow Swiss
- Mac bare metal with Intel, M1, M1 Max and M2 Pro only; from CHF 0.27/h or CHF 199/month; 24-hour minimum, hourly
  billing; Screen Sharing/ARD/VNC plus a web KVM; root [S19]. No M4/M6, and more expensive than MacStadium.

### 2.7 AWS EC2 Mac
- Types: mac2 (M1/16 GB), mac2-m2 (24 GB), mac2-m2pro (32 GB), **mac-m4 (M4/24 GB)**, **mac-m4pro (M4 Pro/48 GB)**,
  mac-m4max (128 GB), mac-m3ultra (256 GB); M4 hosts include a 2 TB instance store [S1].
- us-east-1 on-demand (AWS Pricing API, July 2026): mac2 $0.65/h, mac2-m2 $0.878/h, mac2-m2pro $1.56/h, **mac-m4
  $1.23/h (≈ $898/month)**, **mac-m4pro $1.97/h (≈ $1,438/month)** [S2]. Savings Plans up to 44% off with 3-year
  commitment [S1]. EBS gp3 extra: $0.08/GB-month, $0.005/IOPS-month above 3,000, $0.06/MB/s-month above 125 [S6]; AWS
  recommends 10,000 IOPS / 400 MiB/s for Mac [S3] → a 500 GB root ≈ $40 + $35 + $16.5 ≈ **$92/month**.
- Constraints [S3][S4][S5]:
  - Dedicated Host only, **24-hour minimum allocation**, one instance per host; billing is per host.
  - Boots from EBS; internal SSD is not bootable; **FileVault must not be enabled** (host fails to boot) [S3][S5].
    (Auto-login needs FileVault off anyway [S30].)
  - **Stop/terminate triggers a scrub of up to 4.5 h on Apple silicon** (not billed, but the host is unavailable);
    stop itself takes ~15 min; NVRAM resets on stop [S3][S4b][S5].
  - GUI: set a password for ec2-user, enable Screen Sharing with `launchctl`, then connect over an SSH tunnel to 5900;
    resolution adjustable with displayplacer [S4].
  - Audio: "no access to the microphone input"; the built-in VNC server does not carry audio; no statement on an
    output device [S5]. Plan on BlackHole. (Unverified whether the Mac mini's internal speaker shows up.)
  - App Store installs (Xcode) are supported [S5]; Apple ID sign-in for purchased apps isn't documented (it's bare
    metal, so it should work — unverified).
  - Licence-activation risk: a host replacement (auto-recovery) changes the hardware, which can consume a
    Steinberg/Ableton activation.
- Verdict: technically fits; ~3.5× MacStadium's price for the same M4/24 GB, plus operational quirks.

### 2.8 Orka, Tart, UTM, Parallels (macOS VMs) — ruled out
- Logic Pro cannot be installed and run (App Store apps don't run; Mac App Store sign-in unsupported) [S14][S15][S16].
- Orka pricing is quote-based; Small Teams Edition launched at $499/month in 2023 (old) [S21].

### 2.9 GitHub Actions / ephemeral runners — ruled out
- Each GitHub-hosted runner is a new VM per job [S22]; per-machine licence activations (Steinberg, Ableton,
  Apple ID) would be lost every run, and Logic can't run in the VM anyway.

### 2.10 Buy a Mac mini and host it yourself (runner-up)
- Current Mac mini (Apple, fetched 2026-11-05): M6 (12-core, 16/24/32 GB, 256 GB–2 TB) and M5 Pro (24/48/64 GB,
  up to 8 TB); built-in speaker, 3.5 mm headphone jack, HDMI with multichannel audio [S24].
- US prices (announced 2026-08-25, shipped 2026-09-22): M6 16 GB/256 GB **$899**; 16 GB/512 GB $1,099;
  **24 GB/512 GB $1,299**; **32 GB/1 TB $1,799**; M5 Pro 24 GB/512 GB $1,699. Upgrades: +$200 for 24 GB, +$400 for
  32 GB; 512 GB +$200, 1 TB +$500, 2 TB +$1,000 (over the 256 GB base) [S25][S26]. So **24 GB/1 TB ≈ $1,599**.
  Prices rose in 2026 because of memory costs; the M4 base went from $599 to $799 in June [S26].
- Power: M6 idle 4 W, max 70 W [S27] → ≈ 3–10 kWh/month ≈ $1 at typical US residential rates (my estimate).
- US colocation for a customer-owned Mac mini: CyberLynk $49.99/month, free setup, 1 Gbps unmetered, 1 IPv4,
  remote reboot, 30-day money-back (listing added 2025-11-14) [S28]; Colocation America $50/month baseline [S28b].
  macminicolo.net returned an empty page; its old $35/month figure is from years ago and not current [S28c].
- Breakeven vs MacStadium M4.M ($249): $1,599 / $249 ≈ **6.4 months at home**; $1,599 / ($249 − $50) ≈ **8 months in
  colo**. vs Scaleway M4-M (≈ $230): ≈ 7 / 9 months. Plus resale value at the end.
- Gotcha: a new M6 cannot run an older macOS than it shipped with (generally true for new Apple hardware; I did not
  find the M6's exact shipping version — likely macOS 26/27, unverified). Dorico 6.2.31 is the hotfix for macOS 27's Core
  MIDI change [S32], so Dorico is fine on 27; Ableton's support for macOS 27 is unverified [S33].
- Gotcha: you own the reliability (power cuts, home internet). Use `tailscaled` as a system daemon so the box is
  reachable before login, and enable "start up automatically after a power failure".

## 3. Licensing and VM findings

### 3.1 Apple macOS licence (Sequoia SLA, extracted from Apple's PDF) [S7]
- **Leasing (Section 3, "Leasing for Permitted Developer Services")**: a lessor may lease the Apple Software
  "in its entirety" if (i) it is used solely for **Permitted Developer Services**, (ii) **"each lease period must be
  for a minimum period of twenty-four (24) consecutive hours"**, (iii) the lessee has **"sole and exclusive use and
  control of the Apple Software and the Apple-branded hardware"** (lessor may give admin support), and (iv) the lessee
  accepts the terms of preinstalled software. Permitted Developer Services means "continuous integration services,
  including but not limited to software development, building software from source, **automated testing during
  software development**, and running necessary developer tools". Lessors must notify Apple before leasing.
  → Our use (automated testing of barmark's exporter and Stem Shovel's converters) fits that definition.
- The licence applies only to Apple-branded hardware ("For use on Apple-branded Systems") [S7].
- **Virtualization (Section 2B(iii))**: up to **two additional copies** "within virtual operating system environments
  on each Apple-branded computer you own or control that is already running the Apple Software", for development,
  testing, macOS Server, or personal non-commercial use; not for service-bureau/time-sharing except as Section 3
  allows; a lessor may virtualize only a single instance as a provisioning tool [S7].

### 3.2 Apple ID / App Store in Apple-silicon macOS VMs
- macOS 15 Sequoia added Apple Account/iCloud sign-in in VMs, **only when both host and guest run macOS 15+ and the VM
  was freshly created from a macOS 15+ IPSW** (upgraded VMs don't qualify); each VM gets its own identity [S14].
- **Mac App Store sign-in is still not supported in VMs** (Parallels KB, reviewed 2026-07-07) [S15]; Eclectic Light
  (Oct 2025, Tahoe): VMs "can't sign into Apple services, particularly the App Store", and most App Store apps
  "simply refuse to run, even free ones" [S16]. → **Logic Pro needs bare metal.**
- Audio in VMs: Virtualization.framework has a virtio sound device (macOS 12+) that routes to the host's audio [S23]
  (moot given the Logic blocker).

### 3.3 Third-party activations
- **Steinberg (Dorico)**: single-user licence can be active on **3 computers simultaneously**; deactivate via Steinberg
  Activation Manager on that machine; unlimited activate/deactivate toggling (2021–22 Steinberg forum posts;
  no newer official page found) [S34]. Deactivate on the MacBook before release, or keep it as one of three.
- **Ableton Live**: a licence covers **two active computers** owned by the registered user [S35]; extra
  authorizations via support. No Ableton statement about VMs. **Live 12 needs internet for authorization** and is
  officially listed through macOS 15 in the (possibly stale) compatibility guide [S33][S36].
  → Kevin's MacBook + the cloud Mac = both Ableton slots.
- **Logic Pro**: $199.99 one-time, or via Apple Creator Studio $12.99/month [S20]. App Store purchases are tied to
  the Apple ID; signing in on a leased bare-metal Mac is fine as long as it is the lessee's exclusive machine.

### 3.4 macOS automation gotchas (apply to every option)
- **Screen Recording re-approval**: macOS 15 asks users to re-confirm screen-recording permission **monthly**
  ("Allow For One Month"); no user-level opt-out [S37]. macOS 15.1 added the MDM restriction key
  `forceBypassScreenCaptureAlert` (com.apple.applicationaccess), deployable **only via MDM**, with mixed field
  reports [S38]. Whether `/usr/sbin/screencapture` from a launchd agent triggers the prompt is **unverified**. Options:
  enrol the Mac in a cheap/free MDM, or accept a monthly VNC click.
- **Auto-login** is required so Dorico/Ableton/Logic run in a GUI session after reboot; it is unavailable while
  FileVault is on [S30].
- **Tailscale**: the App Store and Standalone apps cannot run before login; the open-source `tailscaled` launch
  daemon can (needed to reach the box after a reboot) [S31].
- **Headless display**: Screen Sharing works without a monitor at a default ~1920×1080; an HDMI dummy plug or a
  virtual-display utility gives other resolutions (community sources) [S29]. Ask hosting providers whether they fit
  dummy plugs.

## 4. Sizing

| Component | RAM | Disk |
|---|---|---|
| Dorico Elements 6 | 8 GB recommended (4 GB min) [S39] | 6–16 GB (retailer figures vary) [S39] |
| Ableton Live 12 | 8 GB min, 16 GB recommended [S36] | ~10 GB base on Apple silicon, up to 76 GB more for sound content [S36] |
| Logic Pro | – (Apple silicon, macOS 15.6+) [S20] | Not stated on Apple's page; the full sound library is tens of GB (unverified) |
| Claude Code + Bun + git checkouts | ~2–4 GB | ~10–20 GB |

- **RAM: 24 GB is enough** to keep Dorico and one DAW open plus an agent; 32 GB gives headroom for both DAWs at once.
  16 GB will swap.
- **Storage: 512 GB minimum, 1 TB comfortable** (macOS ≈ 30–40 GB, apps + content 100–150 GB, test projects/renders).
  256 GB plans are out.
- CPU: a base M4/M6 is ample; M4 Pro only if running many renders in parallel.

## 5. Recommendation

**Primary: MacStadium M4.M — Mac mini M4, 24 GB, 512 GB, bare metal, $249/month, month-to-month** [S8].
- Meets every hard requirement: real macOS GUI session on Apple hardware, Screen Sharing (use over Tailscale),
  screenshots, full root for Claude Code/SSH/installers, persistent single-tenant machine (licences stay activated),
  App Store sign-in for Logic, US location, verified official pricing.
- Estimated monthly cost: **$249** (+ Logic $199.99 one-time or Creator Studio $12.99/month [S20]; Dorico/Ableton
  licences already owned). Upsize to M4.L ($349) if 512 GB proves tight.

**Runner-up: buy a Mac mini M6 24 GB/1 TB (≈ $1,599) and host it at home/office (≈ $1/month) or in a US colo
(≈ $50/month)** [S25][S27][S28]. Cheaper after ~7–8 months, real audio hardware, full physical control (dummy plug, no
lease rules), resale value. Downsides: capex, you handle hardware/power/network, and it will run macOS 26/27 rather
than 15.

**Also viable:** Scaleway M4-M (€199, 32 GB/1 TB, Paris) [S10][S11] or OakHost M6.M/M4Pro.L (€200/€260, Germany) [S12]
if EU hosting is fine and you want more RAM/storage per dollar. **Not recommended:** EC2 Mac (≈ $990/month all-in),
MacinCloud until bare metal is confirmed, Flow Swiss (old chips), any VM-based option, GitHub runners.

## 6. Open questions for Kevin

1. **Rent or own?** How long will the test Mac be needed? Beyond ~8 months, buying wins on cost.
2. **US or EU hosting?** (MacStadium US vs Scaleway/OakHost EU.) Latency matters little for automation but a bit for VNC.
3. **Which macOS version?** Stay on macOS 15.x (matches the MacBook; Logic needs ≥ 15.6; Ableton's guide lists up to
   15) or move to 27 (Dorico 6.2.31 already carries the macOS 27 MIDI hotfix)? Rented M4s can likely run 15; a new M6
   cannot.
4. **Logic Pro licence:** buy outright ($199.99) or Creator Studio ($12.99/month), and which Apple ID signs in on a
   machine a provider can access? A dedicated "bench" Apple ID is worth considering.
5. **Licence slots:** OK to use one of 3 Steinberg activations and the 2nd of 2 Ableton authorizations on the cloud Mac?
   (Kevin's MacBook keeps the other.)
6. **Screen-recording prompt:** accept a monthly manual approval over VNC, or enrol the Mac in an MDM to set
   `forceBypassScreenCaptureAlert`?
7. **Audio needs:** do the DAW tests need real audio output, or just a device to open projects/export? (BlackHole
   suffices for the latter.) Ask the host whether the Mac mini's internal speaker shows up in CoreAudio.
8. Ask MacStadium before ordering: which macOS image, can they install 15.x, is an HDMI dummy plug fitted, and is
   there a setup fee or minimum term for M4.M.
